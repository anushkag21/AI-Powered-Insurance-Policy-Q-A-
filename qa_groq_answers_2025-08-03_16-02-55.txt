q4) Can you explain the details of the Emergency Accidental Hospitalization cover?
ans) Emergency Accidental Hospitalisation Cover – Key Details  

1. Purpose: Reimburses “reasonable and customary charges” for emergency medical expenses incurred as an **In-Patient** in India, when the insured needs **immediate treatment** solely because of an **accidental injury**.  

2. What is paid for (up to the Sum Insured shown in the policy schedule):  
   • In-patient hospitalisation at the nearest facility where the accident occurs.  
   • X-ray, diagnostic tests and any other medically necessary investigations **during the stay**.  
   • Prescribed therapies (heat therapy, physiotherapy, photo therapy, etc.) that require in-patient care.  
   • Automatic **7-day extension** of cover **after the policy expiry** if the insured is still medically unfit to travel home and the Assistance Service Provider has been informed; treatment for any **new** illness/injury that arises after the expiry date is **not** covered.  

3. Important conditions:  
   • Benefits are payable only while the insured is an **In-Patient** for the accidental injury.  
   • All claims are subject to the overall Sum Insured, any sub-limits, deductibles and the policy’s general exclusions.

q5) What is covered under the Personal Accident section, and what are the compensation limits for death and disability?

q8) How does the policy cover theft or loss of baggage and personal belongings?
ans) The policy covers loss or theft of baggage and personal belongings under two specific endorsements:

1. **Baggage Loss in Common Carrier on Benefit Basis (Airways)** – If the entire piece of checked-in baggage is lost due to **theft, misdirection, or non-delivery** by the airline (Common Carrier), the policy pays a **fixed benefit equal to the sum insured** stated in the schedule, over and above the base sum insured. A **Property Irregularity Report (PIR)** from the airline is mandatory.

2. **Loss of Baggage and Personal Belongings** – This covers **personal effects** (mobile, laptop, tablet) and **personal documents** (identity card, passport, driving license, etc.) that are **lost or damaged** during the insured journey. The policy reimburses the **replacement cost** up to the total sum insured, after applying any deductible.

**Key Conditions:**
- Immediate written notice must be given to the airline (for transit loss) or the police (for theft).
- A copy of the **airline or police report** is required to process the claim.
- **Valuables, money, tickets, prohibited items, and damage due to delay or confiscation by authorities are excluded.**
- Claims are assessed based on the age and wear-and-tear of the lost items.
- If benefits have already been paid under other baggage-specific covers, those amounts are deducted from this claim.

So, theft or loss of baggage and personal belongings is **covered**, subject to the above terms and exclusions.

9. **Congenital conditions** – Claims related to congenital external diseases, defects, or anomalies are excluded.

10. **Deductibles** – Claims are subject to deductibles as specified in the policy schedule, which may result in partial or full rejection of certain claims.

These exclusions are clearly outlined in the policy documents and apply to all claims under this travel insurance policy.
