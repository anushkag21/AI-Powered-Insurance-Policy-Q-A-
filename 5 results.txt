q1) Who are eligible to be insured under this group travel insurance policy?
ans) The persons eligible to be insured under this Group Domestic Travel Insurance policy are those who belong to a formally constituted “Group.”  
According to the policy wording, a Group is defined as persons who come together with a common purpose or common economic activity.  Examples explicitly listed include:

• Employees of a company (or employee-welfare associations)  
• Members of a co-operative society  
• Groups covered by Government bodies for identifiable sets of individuals  
• Credit- or debit-card / Kisan-credit-card holders insured through the card-issuing company  
• Customers of a particular business, professional association, bank borrowers or depositors, or customers of a bank or aggregator, or any similar cohort administered by a group administrator where insurance is provided as an add-on benefit.

Hence, any individual falling within such a group arrangement is eligible to be covered under this group travel insurance policy.

q4) Can you explain the details of the Emergency Accidental Hospitalization cover?
ans) Emergency Accidental Hospitalisation is a Base Cover under the policy.  It reimburses the Reasonable and Customary Charges for emergency medical expenses incurred in India when the insured is admitted as an In-Patient following an accidental injury, up to the Sum Insured shown in the Policy Schedule.

What is covered  
•  In-patient treatment in a local hospital where the insured is staying at the time of the accident.  
•  X-rays, diagnostic tests and other reasonable diagnostic costs during hospitalisation.  
•  Heat therapy, physiotherapy, photo therapy or similar treatments prescribed by a medical practitioner that require in-patient care.  
•  If the insured is still medically unfit to travel after the policy expires, the insurer automatically extends cover for a further 7 days (provided the Assistance Service Provider is notified and transport home is not possible).  Any new illness or injury that arises after the policy period is not covered.

What is NOT covered (Special Exclusions)  
•  Treatments that could reasonably be postponed until the insured returns home.  
•  Charges above reasonable and customary limits.  
•  Cosmetic or plastic surgery unless medically required for accident/burn injuries.  
•  Expenses for rest, recuperation or rehabilitation at spas, nursing homes, sanatoriums, etc.  
•  Pregnancy, childbirth or related consequences.

Each claim under this cover is subject to a Deductible shown in the Policy Certificate.

q5) What is covered under the Personal Accident section, and what are the compensation limits for death and disability?
ans) The Personal Accident section provides compensation for accidental death and permanent total disablement (PTD) as follows:

• **Accidental Death (AD):** 100 % of the Sum Insured is payable to the nominee or legal representative.  
• **Permanent Total Disablement (PTD):** 100 % of the Sum Insured is also payable when the insured suffers any of the listed irrecoverable losses (e.g., loss of both eyes, loss of two entire hands or feet, total paralysis, etc.).

The Sum Insured stated in the Policy Schedule is the maximum liability of the insurer for any single claim under these benefits.

q6) How does the policy cover outpatient (OPD) treatment in emergencies?
ans) The policy covers outpatient (OPD) treatment in an emergency only when it arises from an accidental injury. The benefit reimburses reasonable and customary charges up to the sum-insured limit shown in the policy schedule, and a separate deductible applies to each claim. Coverage is explicitly restricted to accidents; routine or non-accidental OPD visits are not included, and any treatment that could reasonably wait until the insured returns home is excluded.

q8) How does the policy cover theft or loss of baggage and personal belongings?
ans) The policy provides two distinct but complementary covers for theft or loss of baggage and personal belongings:

1. Checked-in Baggage – Benefit Basis (Airways)  
   Endorsement 43A pays a fixed benefit, equal to the sum-insured shown in the schedule, if the entire piece of checked-in baggage is lost while in the custody of a common carrier (airline) due to theft, mis-direction or non-delivery at the destination.  
   • The claim must be supported by a Property Irregularity Report (PIR) from the airline.  
   • The benefit is payable in addition to the base sum insured and is capped at the amount stated in the schedule for all checked-in baggage.  
   • No payment is made for valuables, money, securities, tickets, or for partial loss or damage to the contents.

2. Loss of Baggage and Personal Belongings (Endorsement 44)  
   If personal documents (identity cards, passport, driving licence, etc.) or personal effects (mobile, laptop, tablet) belonging to the insured are lost or damaged during the journey, the policy will reimburse the cost of replacement up to the Total Sum Insured shown in the schedule, subject to any deductible.  
   • Claims must be reported immediately to the relevant common carrier (for in-transit loss) or to the police (for theft), and a copy of the report must accompany the claim.  
   • Original purchase receipts or, for jewellery, pre-journey valuation certificates must be submitted.  
   • Wear-and-tear, cash/banknotes, traveller cheques, tickets, breakdown of fragile items, unaccompanied baggage and losses recoverable from other insurers are excluded.

Both sections operate independently; however, if a payment has already been made under the optional “Baggage Loss in Common Carrier (Airways)” or similar cover, the amount paid will be set off against any claim under Endorsement 44.

q9) What is the coverage for travel delay and missed connections?
ans) Answer:

Travel-delay and missed-connection benefits are provided under two separate optional covers.

1. Trip Delay (Airways) – reimbursement basis  
   • Reimburses reasonable additional meal and lodging expenses if the insured’s trip is delayed beyond the number of hours stated in the policy certificate, and only when the delay is caused by:  
     – airline strike (with advance booking),  
     – inclement weather, or  
     – the destination/accommodation becoming uninhabitable due to fire, flood, vandalism, burglary or natural disaster.  
   • Subject to a deductible and production of receipts.

2. Missed Connection (Airways) – benefit basis  
   • A fixed sum-insured benefit is paid if a confirmed onward connecting flight is missed at the transfer point because the incoming confirmed scheduled flight arrived late and no onward transportation is provided within 2/3 hours of the actual arrival time.  
   • The delay must be certified in writing by the airline.  
   • A separate deductible applies to each claim.

Both benefits are “over and above the Base Sum Insured”.

q12) What are the steps an insured must follow in case of loss or theft of checked baggage?
ans) In the event of loss or theft of checked baggage, the insured must follow these steps:

1. **Notify immediately in writing**  
   - If the loss or damage occurs **in transit**, notify the relevant **Common Carrier** (e.g., airline).  
   - If the loss or theft occurs **outside transit**, notify the **local police authority**.

2. **Obtain and submit documentation**  
   - Obtain a **Common Carrier report** (e.g., Property Irregularity Report from the airline) or **police report**, depending on where the loss occurred.  
   - Submit a **copy of this report** when filing the claim.

3. **Retain and submit original documents**  
   - Keep the **original tickets and baggage slips** and submit them with the claim if the loss is by a Common Carrier.  
   - For goods purchased during the insured journey, submit **original purchase receipts**.  
   - For jewellery, submit **original or certified valuation certificates** issued **before** the trip.

These steps are essential to ensure that the claim is processed under the policy.

q15) What are common reasons for claim rejection under this policy?
ans) Based on the policy wording provided, the following are the most common grounds on which a claim under this travel policy will be rejected:

1. Pre-existing conditions  
   Any medical condition that existed before the policy period, and any complication arising from it, is excluded. The policy explicitly states it “is not designed to provide an indemnity with respect to medical services, the need for which arises out of a pre-existing condition.”

2. Travelling against medical advice or with a terminal prognosis  
   Claims will be denied if the insured is:  
   • travelling against the advice of a Medical Practitioner;  
   • on a waiting list for declared medical treatment; or  
   • has received a terminal prognosis for any medical condition.

3. Engaging in restricted or hazardous activities  
   • Professional or semi-professional sports, adventure sports (unless declared and an extra premium paid), or any sporting activity involving training or competition.  
   • Participation in naval, military or air-force operations.  
   • Specific pursuits such as skydiving, bungee jumping, scuba diving, mountaineering, racing, etc., unless specifically covered.

4. War, civil commotion, nuclear risks and related perils  
   Any loss caused by war, civil war, rebellion, insurrection, riots, nuclear radiation or contamination, and similar events is excluded.

5. Breach of policy conditions or fraudulent claims  
   • Failure to satisfy all policy terms and conditions (a “condition precedent” to any payment).  
   • Any claim supported by fraudulent statements or devices will lead to forfeiture of all benefits.

6. Specific exclusions found in the Accidental Death & Disability section  
   • Death or injury arising from intentional self-injury, suicide, or attempted suicide.  
   • Occurrences while under the influence of alcohol or drugs.  
   • Injuries sustained while committing or attempting a criminal act.  
   • Accidents connected with operating an aircraft (except as a fare-paying passenger in a scheduled multi-engine commercial flight).  
   • Injuries resulting from terrorism or terrorist activities.

7. Travel outside the scope of the policy  
   • Any claim if the insured person is not travelling as a passenger on a properly licensed carrier.  
   • Claims for incidents occurring outside the period of insurance or outside the geographic scope (India only).

These exclusions are explicitly set out in the policy wording and represent the principal reasons claims under this Group Domestic Travel Insurance policy would be rejected.

q17) Does the policy cover pre-existing diseases or conditions?
ans) No – the policy does **not** cover pre-existing diseases or conditions in the ordinary course of treatment.  

The wording is explicit:  

> “This policy is not designed to provide an indemnity with respect to medical services, the need for which arises out of a pre-existing condition …”

q19) What are the deductible limits, and how do they apply?
ans) The deductible limits are specified in the Policy Schedule / Policy Certificate for each benefit.  
They are applied on a per-event (per-incident) basis: for every separate claim the insurer will withhold the stated amount, percentage, number of days or hours before any benefits become payable.  
The deductible does not reduce the Sum Insured and must be satisfied each time a claim is made, even if multiple claims arise under the same benefit.

q20) What happens in case of policy cancellation during the period?
ans) In the event of policy cancellation during the policy period, the following applies:

1. **Cancellation by the Company**  
   - The insurer may cancel an annual policy at any time by giving 30 days’ written notice via Registered Post.  
   - Cancellation is permitted **only on grounds** of fraud, misrepresentation, non-disclosure of material facts, or non-cooperation by the insured.  
   - If fraud or misrepresentation is established, the policy is **void** from inception and **all premiums paid are forfeited**.  
   - If cancellation is due to non-cooperation, the insurer will refund the unexpired premium calculated using the **short-period scale** below, **provided no claims have been made**:

   | Policy Period Utilised | Premium Retained by Insurer |
   |------------------------|------------------------------|
   | Up to 15%              | 25%                          |
   | Up to 25%              | 50%                          |
   | Up to 50%              | 75%                          |
   | Over 50%               | 100% (no refund)              |

2. **Cancellation by the Policyholder**  
   - The insured may cancel the policy by giving 30 days’ written notice.  
   - The refund is calculated on a **short-period basis** as per the table above.  
   - **No refund** is payable if **any claim has been made** up to the date of cancellation.

3. **Single-Trip Policies**  
   - Early termination is allowed only if the insured returns to their usual residence earlier than the policy end date.  
   - Refund is provided **only for unutilised days exceeding 10 days**, calculated as the difference between the original premium and the premium for the revised shorter duration.

In summary, cancellation terms depend on who initiates the cancellation and the reason. Fraudulent cases lead to forfeiture of premiums, while voluntary cancellations may attract a pro-rata refund based on the duration the policy was in force and subject to no claims.

q24) How are disputes or claim disagreements resolved according to the policy?
ans) Under the Cholamandalam MS General Insurance policy any dispute or disagreement over the quantum of a claim—once liability has been accepted by the insurer—must be resolved through arbitration.

Specifically, the difference is to be referred to a sole arbitrator appointed jointly by the insured and the insurer.  If the parties cannot agree on a single arbitrator within thirty days of either side requesting arbitration, the matter is escalated to a panel of three arbitrators: one appointed by each party and a third appointed by the two chosen arbitrators.  All proceedings are conducted in accordance with the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, and obtaining a final arbitral award is a condition precedent to any court action.  Should the arbitration clause be held invalid, disputes fall under the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in Chennai.

Separate from arbitration, policy-holders also have access to the company’s grievance-redress mechanism and, ultimately, to the Insurance Ombudsman or IRDAI channels, but these address service-related grievances rather than disputes over the amount of an admitted claim.

q25) What assistance services are provided under the policy, especially for emergency medical evacuations?
ans) Under the CHOLAMANDALAM MS Group Domestic Travel Insurance policy, the following assistance services are expressly provided for emergency medical evacuations:

1. Emergency Medical Evacuation  
   • The policy will reimburse reasonable transportation charges if the insured person needs to be moved from one hospital to another nearest hospital, or from the last treating hospital back to his / her usual place of residence.  
   • The evacuation must be necessitated by an emergency accident (pre-existing diseases are excluded) and must be formally prescribed by the treating Medical Practitioner or the Assistance Service Provider.

2. Repatriation of Mortal Remains  
   • If death occurs, the policy covers the cost of returning the insured person’s mortal remains to the place of residence, including related preparation, packing and transportation expenses.

In both cases, the evacuation or repatriation must be arranged through or approved by the Assistance Service Provider, and all supporting documentation (medical reports, transportation details, original bills, etc.) will be required for reimbursement.

q27) What coverage is provided for pets traveling with the insured?
ans) Coverage for pets travelling with the insured is provided under two complementary endorsements in the policy:

1. Travel with Pet Cover (Endorsement no. 39)  
   • If the pet suffers an accidental injury or illness (excluding pre-existing conditions) during the trip, the policy reimburses the actual medical expenses incurred for in-patient or out-patient treatment.

Both benefits are payable in addition to the base sum insured and are subject to the pet being transported/kept in accordance with carrier and accommodation rules and being kept solely for companionship, protection or recreation (not for commercial or research purposes).

q28) How is hijack distress allowance triggered, and what compensation is provided?
ans) The Hijack Distress Allowance is triggered when the common carrier in which the insured person is travelling is hijacked during a trip covered under the policy. The allowance is paid for each hour the carrier remains under hijack, after applying the deductible (in hours) specified in the policy schedule.

Compensation is provided as an allowance for every full hour of hijack, subject to the deductible and the limits shown in the policy schedule/certificate. The benefit is paid over and above the base sum insured.

q29) What coverage is available for loss or misuse of debit or credit cards during travel?
ans) The policy provides specific coverage for loss or misuse of debit/credit/forex cards while the insured person is on a domestic trip within India.  

Under **Endorsement No. 33 – Debit/Credit Card Fraud**, the insurer will reimburse the financial loss arising from **fraudulent utilization** of the card **after** it has been reported lost or stolen to the issuing bank, **up to the time the card is blocked**. The reimbursement is capped at the **Sum Insured** listed in the policy schedule.

To qualify for a claim, the following conditions must be met:  
- The loss or theft must be **reported to the issuing bank as soon as practicable**.  
- A **written police report** must be submitted to the insurer.  
- The insured person must have taken **reasonable steps** to prevent the loss.  
- Claims are payable **only in India and in Indian Rupees**.  
A **deductible**, as stated in the policy certificate, applies to each claim.

Key exclusions include:  
- Losses recoverable from another source.  
- Claims where bank reporting procedures were not followed promptly.  
- Losses when the card was left unattended.  
- Costs for card replacement.  
- Any misuse **after** the card was reported lost or stolen.
